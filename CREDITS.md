# Credits and licences

## Card artwork

The 52 card faces and the suit icons are drawn for this project by `Tools/gen_cards.py` (jumbo-index layout, suits drawn as shapes, rank letters
in Noto Serif Bold, SIL OFL) and are released as CC0. Earlier versions used the LGPL "Vector Playing Cards 3.2" deck by Chris Aguilar
(https://totalnonsense.com/open-source-vector-playing-cards/); none of its artwork remains in the game.

## Fonts

Cinzel and Noto Serif (bold), and Amiri Bold for the Arabic text (The Amiri Project Authors, https://github.com/aliftype/amiri; licence text in
`Assets/Core Game Assets/Fonts/Amiri-OFL.txt`), all SIL Open Font License 1.1, from Google Fonts.

## Interface design

The gameplay screen layout, colour palette, portraits and table art come from the "Tarneeb VIP - Unity Gameplay UI" Figma file, imported with a
third-party Figma importer that is no longer part of the project. The player portraits are cropped by
`Tools/gen_portraits.py`; icons, jewels, buttons and panels are redrawn by `Tools/gen_ui_art.py`.

## Portraits

All four portraits are AI-generated with the free Pollinations image service (FLUX); see DEVLOG.md for the
prompts. Check the service's terms before publishing, or replace them with art you own.

## Emoji (CC0, third party)

The twelve chat emoji are "Emoji Set" by SunKing2 on Openclipart (https://openclipart.org/detail/334187/emoji-set), public domain / CC0. The source
SVG is kept in `Tools/design/emoji_openclipart_334187.svg`; `Tools/gen_emoji.py` rasterises and slices it into `Resources/Emoji/emoji_atlas.png`.

## Original assets (CC0)

Icons, particles, all sound effects and both music loops are generated by the scripts in `Tools/` (`gen_ui_art.py`, `gen_card_back.py`,
`gen_audio.py`) and are released as CC0.

## Multiplayer

Photon PUN 2 (Exit Games) is not part of this repository; import it from the Asset Store (see `Assets/Core Game Assets/Scripts/Net/README.md`).

## Game icon

Drawn by `Tools/gen_icon.py` from the project's own spade and crown sprites (CC0).
