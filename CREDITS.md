## Interface design

The gameplay screen layout, colour palette, portraits and table art come from the "Tarneeb VIP - Unity Gameplay UI" Figma file, imported with the
Krykftn "Figma To Unity" importer (`Assets/FigmaToUnity`, third-party, see its README for its licence). The player portraits are retouched by
`Tools/gen_portraits.py`; icons, jewels, buttons and panels are redrawn by `Tools/gen_ui_art.py`.

## Original assets (CC0)

Emoji, icons, particles, all sound effects and both music loops are generated by the scripts in `Tools/` (`gen_emoji.py`, `gen_ui_art.py`,
`gen_card_back.py`, `gen_audio.py`) and are released as CC0.
